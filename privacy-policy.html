Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Growvelt collects, uses, shares, protects, and manages personal information across our courses, job platform, applications, employer tools, payments, emails, and support services.

Last updated: July 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when you create an account, sign in, apply for jobs, save jobs, create job alerts, post jobs, request employer verification, register for courses, make payments, subscribe to updates, contact support, or use our forms.

This may include your name, email address, phone number, location, professional details, resume or CV, profile information, application answers, job preferences, company name, company website, professional email, LinkedIn profile, business location, messages, uploaded files, and support requests.

2. Account, Authentication, and Profile Data

When you sign in with email, password, Google, or another supported authentication method, we may process account identifiers, email addresses, profile names, avatar images, login timestamps, and authentication status so we can create and secure your account.

If you use third-party sign-in, the provider may share limited account information with us according to your settings and the provider's privacy policy.

3. Growvelt Ambassador Program

When you apply to the Growvelt Ambassador Program, we may process your account or user identifier, account role, Ambassador application responses, motivation, selected promotion channels, optional social or professional profile URL, contribution plan, application status, Ambassador cohort or recognition where applicable, and submission or review timestamps.

To review and manage Ambassador participation, we may also process internal administrative information such as review status, review timestamps, internal review notes, status or lifecycle history, and relevant reviewer or account identifiers. Internal review notes are not shown to applicants by default.

We may use account contact information to provide Ambassador-related communications, including application-related updates where applicable, approval, rejection, suspension, reinstatement, revocation, and important Program updates.

Existing Growvelt referral information may be displayed or used as part of the Ambassador experience. The Program reuses existing referral infrastructure where applicable rather than requiring an unrelated referral identity, and existing Privacy Policy treatment of referral and account data continues to apply.

We may remember whether a user has dismissed or acknowledged an Ambassador Program announcement so that the same notice does not need to be repeatedly displayed.

We use Ambassador information to review applications; administer participation; manage status and lifecycle; provide Program features and recognition; prevent abuse or fraud; maintain security; communicate decisions and important updates; support applicable referral or reward administration; comply with legal obligations; and improve Program operations consistently with this Privacy Policy.

Ambassador application and review information is not public by default. Authorized Growvelt personnel, systems, and relevant service providers may process it where necessary under the sharing provisions of this Privacy Policy. Where the Program includes public Ambassador recognition or features, limited profile or Ambassador-recognition information may be displayed where appropriate; application answers, internal review notes, and private email or contact information are not made public by default.

Ambassador information is retained under the Data Retention framework in this Privacy Policy. The choices, rights, contact procedures, and security provisions described below continue to apply.

4. Job Applications and Employer Data

When job seekers apply for roles, we process the information needed to submit, track, and update applications. Employers may access application details for jobs they manage, including applicant names, contact details, resumes, answers, and application status.

When employers request verification or post jobs, we process company information to review legitimacy, prevent abuse, approve or reject access, publish jobs, and send related email notifications.

5. Career Assistant and AI Processing

When you use Growvelt Career Assistant, we process the information you submit to provide the requested result. Depending on the tool, this may include resume text, job descriptions, cover-letter details, interview answers, candidate evidence, evaluation criteria, interviewer notes, and generated reports.

Career Assistant may use automated rules and external artificial intelligence service providers. Where supported, Growvelt removes direct contact details such as email addresses, labelled phone numbers, and web links from resume and candidate text before sending that text to an external AI model. This safeguard may not identify every sensitive detail, so users should remove identification numbers, home addresses, medical information, protected characteristics, and any information that is not necessary for the task.

Career Assistant results are generated guidance and may be incomplete or incorrect. They are not legal advice, professional certification, a guarantee of employment, or an automated hiring decision. Employers must have an appropriate right or lawful basis to process candidate information and must use qualified human review.

Growvelt does not add a generated report to your saved-report library unless you choose to save it. Saved Career Assistant reports are associated with your signed-in account, can be deleted from the relevant saved-items page, and are deleted when the related authentication account is deleted, subject to any retention required by law or platform security obligations.

6. Payments and Upgrades

When you purchase an upgrade, course, or paid service, payment details may be processed by third-party payment providers. Growvelt may receive transaction references, payment status, amount, currency, plan type, customer email, and related records needed to confirm purchases and provide services.

We do not intentionally store full card numbers or sensitive banking credentials on our own servers.

7. How We Use Information

  • To provide accounts, courses, job listings, applications, saved jobs, job alerts, employer tools, and support.
  • To review employer verification requests and job posts.
  • To send application updates, job alerts, admin emails, course updates, transactional messages, and support replies.
  • To process payments, upgrades, refunds where applicable, and service access.
  • To improve website performance, security, user experience, content, and platform quality.
  • To detect fraud, spam, fake jobs, account abuse, security incidents, or policy violations.
  • To comply with legal, tax, accounting, regulatory, and dispute-resolution obligations.

8. Cookies, Analytics, and Technical Data

We may collect technical information such as device type, browser, IP address, pages visited, referring pages, session activity, and interaction data. We may use cookies or similar technologies to keep users signed in, remember preferences, improve performance, and understand how people use the website.

9. When We Share Information

We may share information with service providers who help us operate Growvelt, including hosting, database, authentication, storage, artificial intelligence processing, email delivery, analytics, payments, security, and customer support providers. They process information to provide services to Growvelt under their applicable terms and safeguards.

We may share job application information with the relevant employer or recruiter. We may share information if required by law, to protect users, to investigate abuse, to enforce our terms, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition.

We do not sell personal information as a standalone product.

10. External Links and Partner Jobs

Growvelt may link to external employer websites, partner job boards, payment pages, social media pages, or third-party resources. Their privacy practices are controlled by them, not Growvelt. Please review their policies before submitting personal information.

11. Data Retention

We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide services, maintain accounts, support applications, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce our terms. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of data and the purpose for which it is used.

Career Assistant input is processed to return the requested output. Growvelt does not intentionally place unsaved input or output in your saved-report library. Infrastructure and AI service providers may temporarily process technical records or request data for security, reliability, and abuse prevention according to their applicable policies and our service configuration.

12. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so users should also protect their passwords, devices, and account access.

13. Your Choices and Rights

You may update certain account details, manage preferences, unsubscribe from optional emails, or contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal information where applicable by law.

You may delete individual Career Assistant reports or clear your saved Career Assistant library from the relevant workspace. Deleting your Growvelt account also removes Career Assistant saved items linked to that authentication account.

Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, payment, anti-fraud, or legitimate business reasons.

14. Children's Privacy

Growvelt services are not intended for children who are not legally able to use online services without appropriate consent. If we learn that we collected information from a child in a way that violates applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to delete or restrict it.

15. International Users

By using Growvelt, you understand that your information may be processed in countries where our service providers, hosting systems, or business operations are located. We take reasonable steps to protect information according to this policy.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements, or business practices. The updated version will be posted with a revised date.

17. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at support@growvelt.com or through the contact page on the Growvelt website.
