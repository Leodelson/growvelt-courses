## Batch-to-bank reconciliation preparation

Paystack's [Settlements API](https://paystack.com/docs/api/settlement/) describes settlement batches as payouts to the merchant bank account and provides the transactions that make up each batch. Its [payout guide](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2125314) describes the payout amount as the included payments less transaction fees, with deductions shown in payout activity. Therefore the bank match is one record per settlement batch, not one bank deposit per course purchase.

The prepared migration `20261013000000` and Learning Admin control record a private, append-only manual match: Paystack settlement ID/date and `effective_amount`, observed bank credit amount/date, statement-line reference, and reviewing admin. The server re-fetches the successful Live settlement and the linked company charge and rejects an amount mismatch; the database requires a prior verified charge in that batch and exact bank/Paystack amount equality. The bank statement itself is not machine-verified or uploaded—the authorized admin is attesting that they inspected the actual statement. A discrepancy remains unrecorded and must be investigated outside the release path.

This is evidence only. Keep `PAYMENTS_LIVE_COMPANY_BANK_RECONCILIATION_ENABLED` false until the migration has been applied and reviewed. A reconciled batch updates the release assessment to `bank_settlement_reconciled_release_writer_not_enabled`; it does not release/reserve earnings, enable live company checkout, or initiate a transfer. The seller-release writer and its independent controls are still required.
